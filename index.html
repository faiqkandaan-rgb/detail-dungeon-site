    What the film is

    Four layers thick.
    Only one of them you see.

    Paint protection film is not a sticker. It is a built sheet, and every layer in it is doing a different job between a stone and your paint.

    Self-healing topcoatElastomeric. Light swirls close with heat from the sun or warm water.
    Layer 01
    Urethane filmThe body of the sheet. This is what takes the stone and spreads the hit.
    Layer 02
    Acrylic adhesiveBonds to the clearcoat and releases cleanly years later without lifting paint.
    Layer 03
    Your paintCorrected first, then sealed under the film. Untouched from that day on.

    Interior protection

    The trim scratches first.
    Film it before the keys do.

    Piano black, carbon and brushed trim swirl within months. A clear film, cut to the shape of each piece, goes over the console, the gear surround and the door inserts. You cannot see it. Keys, rings and nails now land on film, not trim.
